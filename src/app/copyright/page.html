2. Fair Use Guidelines

ClipGenius facilitates the creation of short-form video clips from longer source material. We believe that transformative use of copyrighted material for purposes such as commentary, criticism, education, and entertainment may qualify as fair use under Section 107 of the U.S. Copyright Act.

FactorConsideration
Purpose and characterTransformative use (adding new expression, meaning, or message) is more likely to be fair use.
Nature of the workUse of factual or published works is more likely to be fair use than use of creative or unpublished works.
Amount usedUsing only the amount necessary to achieve your transformative purpose weighs in favor of fair use. We limit clips to short durations (typically 15-60 seconds).
Effect on marketIf your use does not substitute for the original work or harm its market, this factor weighs in favor of fair use.

✅ Examples of transformative uses that may qualify as fair use:

  • Commentary and criticism
  • Parody and satire
  • Educational and instructional content
  • News reporting
  • Research and scholarship
  • Meme creation and internet culture

❌ Examples of uses that are unlikely to qualify as fair use:

  • Simply reposting or re-uploading content without transformation
  • Using substantial portions of a creative work
  • Using content in a way that substitutes for the original
  • Using content that harms the market for the original work
